06 Questions

Good questions we get asked.

Will you change our ERP?

No. Your ERP stays exactly as it is. We connect to it through its supported interfaces.

Is our data safe?

Data moves over secure connections, with access limited to what the integration needs.

What if a sync fails?

You get alerted straight away, and the log shows exactly which record and why.

Can you work with custom systems?

Yes, anything with an API, a database or even a scheduled file export.
