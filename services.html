Web Apps

Explore Web Apps in detail →
4 to 8 weeksFixed price
What it is

A complete web application built around how your customers or your team actually work. A customer portal, a booking or ordering system, a marketplace, or a new product taken from idea to a first release real users can pay for.

What's included
  • Product and screen design, so it looks and feels like it belongs to your brand
  • A secure database and sign in, with the right access for every kind of user
  • Works on phone, tablet and desktop from the same app
  • Payments, email and notifications wired in where you need them
  • Hosting set up and handed over, so you own everything we build

Ideal for

Businesses launching a new product or replacing a clunky portal their customers complain about.

Not for

Large platforms needing dozens of features on day one. We will help you find the first release worth launching, then build the rest in stages.

Internal Tools

Explore Internal Tools in detail →
2 to 4 weeksFixed price
What it is

The process your team runs on spreadsheets, email and chat, rebuilt as one proper app. Requests, approvals, inspections, registers and trackers, each with a clear owner, deadlines that watch themselves, and a full history of who did what.

What's included
  • A workflow built around your real process, not a generic template stretched to fit
  • Role based access, so each person sees exactly what they should act on
  • Alerts and escalations, so nothing waits quietly on someone's desk
  • Usable from a phone on site or a desktop in the office
  • Documentation your team can hand to a new hire without calling us

Ideal for

A team with one clearly painful process and someone who can approve the scope.

Not for

Company wide rollouts touching every department at once. We split those into stages, each scoped and priced on its own.

Integrations

Explore Integrations in detail →
2 to 4 weeksFixed price
What it is

The systems you already pay for, finally working together. We connect your ERP, CRM, HR, finance and third party tools, including Oracle Fusion, SAP, Salesforce and Zoho, so information is entered once and appears everywhere it is needed.

What's included
  • A documented connection between your systems, tested against your real data
  • Checks that stop incomplete or wrong data before it reaches your records
  • Scheduled or instant syncing, whichever the process needs
  • A clear record of what moves where, and a plan if anything ever needs rolling back

Ideal for

Teams retyping the same information into two or three systems every day.

Not for

Replacing or reconfiguring your ERP itself. That is a job for your ERP partner, and we will say so.

Dashboards

Explore Dashboards in detail →
1 to 2 weeksFixed price
What it is

Your data, wherever it lives today, in a database, spreadsheets or another system, turned into one live view your team actually opens, instead of a report someone compiles by hand every week.

What's included
  • One dashboard built around the handful of numbers that really come up in your reviews
  • A live connection to your data, with no manual export in the middle
  • Different views for different roles, from team leads to the board
  • A short walkthrough so anyone can read it without a legend

Ideal for

Teams spending hours building a report before every meeting it is meant to inform.

Not for

A full business intelligence platform with dozens of connected reports. That is a larger project, worth scoping on its own.

Pricing

How pricing actually works

Every project is fixed price, agreed in writing before work starts. We quote after a short scoping call, once we understand what you need, not before. No hourly billing and no open ended retainer.
