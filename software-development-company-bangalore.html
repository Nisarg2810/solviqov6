What we keep seeing in Bangalore

Bengaluru teams are not short of engineers, they are short of engineering time. The work that gets starved is always the internal work: the ops dashboard, the admin panel, the onboarding tool. It sits in the backlog for two quarters while the product team ships product, and operations keeps running on a spreadsheet nobody owns.

What we build for Bangalore businesses

An internal admin and ops console

The back office your product team has not had time to build, done properly, without taking a sprint from the roadmap.

A customer onboarding tracker

A lab or device data dashboard

Questions we get from Bangalore

We have our own engineers. Why would we use you?

Because your engineers should be on the product. We take the internal tool that has been in the backlog for two quarters, build it in four weeks at a fixed price, and hand you the repository. If you want to maintain it yourself afterwards, that is the point.

How much does custom software cost in Bangalore?

Most of our projects land between four and twelve lakh rupees depending on scope. We quote a single fixed figure after a short scoping call, in writing, before any work starts. No hourly billing and no change request invoices mid project.

How long does a project take?

An internal tool is two to four weeks. A full web application is four to eight. We commit to a date in writing at the start, and you get a working link every Friday so you can see it happening rather than take our word for it.

Do we own the code?

Yes, completely. The repository sits in your name, the hosting is in your accounts, and the database exports any time you ask. Nothing we build needs us in order to keep running.
