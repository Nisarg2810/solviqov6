Keep paying for seats, or build it once?

Most tools look cheap per seat and expensive at renewal. This puts licences, annual rises and the hours your team spends working around the tool against one custom build, and shows the month the lines cross.

Your current tool, honestly

Use the tool that covers this process today. If a spreadsheet covers it, put zero seats and count the workaround hours.

02 How to read it

The seat price is rarely the real cost.

01

Renewals compound

A tool at eight percent a year costs half as much again by year five, for the same features.

02

Workarounds are rent too

The hours spent exporting and re keying are part of what the subscription costs you, they are just paid in salary.

03

Owning has upkeep

A custom build is not free after launch. We put realistic maintenance in the maths rather than pretending it is zero.
